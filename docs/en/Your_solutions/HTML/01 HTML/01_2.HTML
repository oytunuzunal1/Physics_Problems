Range Optimization

Discover how launch angle affects the total distance. The mathematical maximum is reached at exactly 45°.

50 m/s

Velocity is kept constant to isolate the effect of the angle.

45°

Mathematical Proof

R = (v₀² · sin(2θ)) / g

For v₀ = 50 m/s:

  • Maximum range occurs when sin(2θ) = 1
  • This means 2θ = 90°, so θ = 45°
  • Absolute Max Range: 254.84 m
