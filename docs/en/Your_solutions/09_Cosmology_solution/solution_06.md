# 6. Galactic Collision

Distance to Andromeda:

d ≈ 2.5 million light-years

1 ly ≈ 9.46 × 10¹² km

d ≈ 2.37 × 10¹⁹ km

Approach speed:

v ≈ 110 km/s

Time:

t = d / v

t ≈ 2.15 × 10¹⁷ s

Convert to years:

t ≈ 6.8 × 10⁹ years

Answer:
The Milky Way and Andromeda will collide in about 6.8 billion years.
