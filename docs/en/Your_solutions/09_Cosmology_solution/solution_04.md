# 4. Hubble's Law

Given:

v = H₀ d

H₀ ≈ 70 (km/s)/Mpc

At the speed of light:

c ≈ 300,000 km/s

Distance:

d = c / H₀

d = 300,000 / 70 ≈ 4286 Mpc

Convert to light-years:

1 Mpc ≈ 3.26 million light-years

d ≈ 4286 × 3.26 million ly

d ≈ 14 billion light-years

Answer:
A galaxy would recede at the speed of light at a distance of about 14 billion light-years.
