# 2. Cosmic Temperatures

We compare the approximate temperatures of different cosmic objects and stages of the universe.

## Explanation

### Universe at nucleosynthesis era
Temperature was about:

T ≈ 10⁹ K

This is the hottest item in the list.

---

### Core of the Sun
The Sun’s core reaches about:

T ≈ 1.5 × 10⁷ K

Nuclear fusion happens there.

---

### Ionization temperature of hydrogen
Hydrogen atoms ionize at roughly:

T ≈ 10⁴ K

---

### Surface of the Sun
The visible surface temperature is:

T ≈ 5800 K

---

### Universe at recombination era
When atoms first formed:

T ≈ 3000 K

---

### Human body temperature
Average body temperature:

T ≈ 310 K

(37°C)

---

### Interstellar space
Typical temperature:

T ≈ 10–100 K

Very cold, but still warmer than the CMB.

---

### Present Cosmic Microwave Background
Current CMB temperature:

T ≈ 2.7 K

---

### 10-solar-mass black hole
By Hawking radiation, such a black hole has an extremely tiny temperature:

T ≈ 10⁻⁸ K

This is the coldest object on the list.

## Final Order (Hottest → Coldest)

1. Universe at nucleosynthesis era  
2. Core of the Sun  
3. Ionization temperature of hydrogen  
4. Surface of the Sun  
5. Universe at recombination era  
6. Human body temperature  
7. Interstellar space  
8. Present CMB radiation  
9. Temperature of a 10-solar-mass black hole
