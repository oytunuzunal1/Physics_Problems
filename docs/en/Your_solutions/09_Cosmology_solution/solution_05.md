# 5. Galactic Year

Distance from galactic center:

r ≈ 27,000 light-years

1 ly ≈ 9.46 × 10¹² km

r ≈ 2.55 × 10¹⁷ km

Orbit circumference:

C = 2πr

C ≈ 1.60 × 10¹⁸ km

Speed:

v ≈ 220 km/s

Time:

t = C / v

t ≈ 7.3 × 10¹⁵ s

Convert to years:

t ≈ 2.3 × 10⁸ years

Answer:
One galactic year is about 230 million years.
