# 10. Doppler Effect

Given:

v = 5 × 10⁶ m/s

c = 3 × 10⁸ m/s

λ_emitted = 486 nm

## Non-relativistic Doppler Shift

λ_observed = λ_emitted (1 + v/c)

λ_observed = 486 × (1 + 5×10⁶ / 3×10⁸)

λ_observed ≈ 494.1 nm

Answer:
Observed wavelength ≈ 494.1 nm
# 10. Doppler Effect

## Relativistic Doppler Shift

λ_observed = λ_emitted √[(1 + v/c)/(1 - v/c)]

λ_observed ≈ 494.2 nm

Answer:
Observed wavelength ≈ 494.2 nm

Comparison:
The relativistic and non-relativistic results are almost identical because v << c.
