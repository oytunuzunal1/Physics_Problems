# 1. Cosmic Chronology

We arrange the events according to the history of the universe from the earliest stage to the latest.

## Explanation

### 1) Inflation
Inflation happened immediately after the Big Bang. During this phase, the universe expanded extremely rapidly in a tiny fraction of a second.

### 2) Quark-gluon plasma era
After inflation, the universe was still extremely hot and dense. Matter existed as free quarks and gluons because atoms could not yet form.

### 3) Big Bang Nucleosynthesis
As the universe cooled further, protons and neutrons combined to form light nuclei such as hydrogen and helium.

### 4) Recombination
Electrons combined with nuclei to form neutral atoms. At this point, light could travel freely through space, producing the Cosmic Microwave Background (CMB).

### 5) Formation of first galaxies
Hundreds of millions of years later, gravity pulled matter together to form the first stars and galaxies.

## Final Order

1. Inflation  
2. Quark-gluon plasma era  
3. Big Bang Nucleosynthesis  
4. Recombination  
5. Formation of first galaxies
