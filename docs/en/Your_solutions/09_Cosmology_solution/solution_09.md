# 9. Cosmic Expansion

Distance between galaxies:

100 million light-years

Convert to Mpc:

1 Mpc ≈ 3.26 million ly

d ≈ 100 / 3.26 ≈ 30.7 Mpc

Expansion speed:

v = H₀ d

v ≈ 70 × 30.7

v ≈ 2149 km/s

Time:

1 billion years

Increase in distance:

Δd = vt

Δd ≈ 7.2 million light-years

Answer:
The distance would increase by about 7 million light-years.
