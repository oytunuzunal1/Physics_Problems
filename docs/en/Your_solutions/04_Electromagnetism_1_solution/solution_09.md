# Task 09 – Vector Lorentz Force

## Problem Statement

Compute magnetic force magnitude using vector cross product.

## Theory

$$
\vec{F} = q \vec{v} \times \vec{B}
$$

## Step-by-Step Solution

$$
\vec{v} = (2, -4, 1), \quad \vec{B} = (1, 2, -1)
$$

Cross product:

$$
\vec{v} \times \vec{B} =
\begin{pmatrix}
\hat{i} & \hat{j} & \hat{k} \\
2 & -4 & 1 \\
1 & 2 & -1
\end{pmatrix}
$$

$$
= (4 - 2)\hat{i} - (-2 - 1)\hat{j} + (4 + 4)\hat{k}
$$

$$
= (2, 3, 8)
$$

Magnitude:

$$
|\vec{F}| = q \sqrt{2^2 + 3^2 + 8^2}
$$

$$
|\vec{F}| = q \sqrt{77}
$$

## Final Result

$$
|\vec{F}| = (1.6 \times 10^{-19}) \sqrt{77}
$$

## Interpretation

Magnetic force depends on perpendicular components of motion.
