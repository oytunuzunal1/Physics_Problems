# Task 07 – Cyclotron Motion

## Problem Statement

An electron accelerated through 5000 V enters a magnetic field $B = 0.1 \, T$. Find radius.

## Theory

Kinetic energy:

$$
\frac{1}{2}mv^2 = eV
$$

Magnetic force provides centripetal force:

$$
\frac{mv^2}{r} = evB
$$

## Step-by-Step Solution

Velocity:

$$
v = \sqrt{\frac{2eV}{m}}
$$

Radius:

$$
r = \frac{mv}{eB}
$$

Substitute:

$$
r \approx 7.5 \times 10^{-3} \, \text{m}
$$

## Final Result

$$
r \approx 7.5 \, \text{mm}
$$

## Interpretation

Electron moves in a small circular path due to magnetic field.
