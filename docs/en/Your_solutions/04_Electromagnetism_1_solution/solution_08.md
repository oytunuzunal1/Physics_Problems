# Task 08 – Lorentz Force

## Problem Statement

A particle enters a magnetic field perpendicular to velocity.

## Theory

Lorentz force:

$$
F = qvB
$$

## Step-by-Step Solution

$$
F = (2 \times 10^{-19})(10^6)(0.5)
$$

$$
F = 1.0 \times 10^{-13} \, \text{N}
$$

## Final Result

$$
F = 1.0 \times 10^{-13} \, \text{N}
$$

## Interpretation

Force depends linearly on charge, velocity, and magnetic field.
