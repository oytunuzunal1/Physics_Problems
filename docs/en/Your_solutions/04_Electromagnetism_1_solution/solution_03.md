# Task 03 – Electrostatic Equilibrium

## Problem Statement

Charges $q_1 = +4C$ and $q_2 = +9C$ are separated by $2 \, \text{m}$.  
Find the position where a third charge $q_3 = +1C$ is in equilibrium.

## Theory

Equilibrium occurs when:

$$
F_1 = F_2
$$

## Step-by-Step Solution

Let distance from $q_1$ be $x$.

$$
k \frac{4}{x^2} = k \frac{9}{(2 - x)^2}
$$

Cancel $k$:

$$
\frac{4}{x^2} = \frac{9}{(2 - x)^2}
$$

Take square root:

$$
\frac{2}{x} = \frac{3}{2 - x}
$$

$$
2(2 - x) = 3x
$$

$$
4 - 2x = 3x
$$

$$
x = \frac{4}{5} = 0.8 \, \text{m}
$$

## Final Result

$$
x = 0.8 \, \text{m from } q_1
$$

## Interpretation

Equilibrium is closer to the smaller charge because the larger charge exerts stronger force.
