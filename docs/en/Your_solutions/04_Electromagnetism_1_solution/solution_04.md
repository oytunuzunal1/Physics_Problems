# Task 04 – Force Comparison

## Problem Statement

Calculate the electric and gravitational forces between an electron and a proton separated by $r = 5.3 \times 10^{-11} \, \text{m}$. Determine the ratio $F_e / F_g$.

## Theory

Electric force:

$$
F_e = k \frac{e^2}{r^2}
$$

Gravitational force:

$$
F_g = G \frac{m_e m_p}{r^2}
$$

## Step-by-Step Solution

Ratio:

$$
\frac{F_e}{F_g} = \frac{k e^2}{G m_e m_p}
$$

Substitute values:

$$
k = 8.99 \times 10^9
$$

$$
G = 6.67 \times 10^{-11}
$$

$$
e = 1.6 \times 10^{-19}
$$

$$
m_e = 9.11 \times 10^{-31}, \quad m_p = 1.67 \times 10^{-27}
$$

$$
\frac{F_e}{F_g} \approx 2.3 \times 10^{39}
$$

## Final Result

$$
\frac{F_e}{F_g} \approx 2.3 \times 10^{39}
$$

## Interpretation

Electric force is enormously stronger than gravity at atomic scales.
