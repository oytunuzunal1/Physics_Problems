# Task 10 – Lorentz Force on a Wire

## Problem Statement

A wire carries current in a magnetic field at different angles.

## Theory

$$
F = BIL \sin \theta
$$

## Step-by-Step Solution

Given:

$$
B = 0.5, \quad I = 10, \quad L = 2
$$

### a) $\theta = 90^\circ$

$$
F = 0.5 \cdot 10 \cdot 2 \cdot 1 = 10 \, \text{N}
$$

### b) $\theta = 45^\circ$

$$
F = 10 \cdot \sin 45^\circ = 10 \cdot \frac{\sqrt{2}}{2}
$$

$$
F \approx 7.07 \, \text{N}
$$

### c) $\theta = 0^\circ$

$$
F = 0
$$

## Final Result

- $10 \, \text{N}$
- $7.07 \, \text{N}$
- $0 \, \text{N}$

## Interpretation

Force depends on angle; maximum at $90^\circ$, zero when parallel.
