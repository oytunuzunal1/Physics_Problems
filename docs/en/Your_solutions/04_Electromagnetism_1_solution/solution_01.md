# Task 01 – Coulomb's Law

## Problem Statement

Four point charges of $+1.0 \, \text{C}$ are placed at the corners of a square of side $1.0 \, \text{m}$.  
A charge of $-2.0 \, \text{C}$ is placed at the center.  
Determine the magnitude and direction of the net electric force acting on the central charge.

## Theory

Coulomb’s law for the force between two point charges:

$$
F = k \frac{|q_1 q_2|}{r^2}
$$

where  
$k = 8.99 \times 10^9 \, \text{N·m}^2/\text{C}^2$

The net force is obtained by vector addition of all forces.

## Step-by-Step Solution

Distance from center to each corner:

$$
r = \frac{\sqrt{2}}{2} \cdot 1 = \frac{1}{\sqrt{2}}
$$

Force from one charge:

$$
F_1 = k \frac{(1)(2)}{r^2}
$$

$$
r^2 = \frac{1}{2}
$$

$$
F_1 = k \cdot \frac{2}{1/2} = 4k
$$

All four forces are equal in magnitude and symmetrically distributed.

Each force points toward a corner (attractive force since charges are opposite).

Due to symmetry, horizontal and vertical components cancel.

## Final Result

$$
F_{\text{net}} = 0
$$

## Interpretation

The system is perfectly symmetric, so all forces cancel.  
The central charge is in equilibrium.
