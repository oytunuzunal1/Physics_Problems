# Task 02 – Electric Potential

## Problem Statement

Charges $+1C$, $-2C$, $+3C$, and $-4C$ are placed at the corners of a square of side $1.0 \, \text{m}$.  
Find the electric potential at the center.

## Theory

Electric potential due to a point charge:

$$
V = k \frac{q}{r}
$$

Potential is a scalar quantity and adds algebraically.

## Step-by-Step Solution

Distance to center:

$$
r = \frac{1}{\sqrt{2}}
$$

Total potential:

$$
V = k \frac{1 - 2 + 3 - 4}{r}
$$

$$
V = k \frac{-2}{1/\sqrt{2}} = -2k\sqrt{2}
$$

## Final Result

$$
V = -2\sqrt{2} \, k
$$

## Interpretation

Negative total charge leads to negative potential at the center.
