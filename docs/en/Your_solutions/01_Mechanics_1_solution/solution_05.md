# Task 05 – Relative Velocity

## Problem Statement

A river flows east with velocity $2 \, \text{m/s}$. A boat moves with speed $5 \, \text{m/s}$ relative to the water and aims to cross directly north.

Determine:

- The heading angle
- The crossing time for a width of $200 \, \text{m}$

## Theory

Relative velocity:

$$
\vec{v}_{\text{ground}} = \vec{v}_{\text{boat}} + \vec{v}_{\text{river}}
$$

To move directly north, the eastward component must cancel.

## Step-by-Step Solution

Let $\theta$ be the angle west of north.

Horizontal balance:

$$
5 \sin \theta = 2
$$

$$
\sin \theta = \frac{2}{5}
$$

$$
\theta \approx 23.6^\circ
$$

Effective northward velocity:

$$
v_y = 5 \cos \theta
$$

$$
v_y \approx 5 \cdot 0.917 = 4.58 \, \text{m/s}
$$

Crossing time:

$$
t = \frac{200}{4.58}
$$

$$
t \approx 43.7 \, \text{s}
$$

## Final Result

- Heading angle: $\theta \approx 23.6^\circ$ west of north  
- Crossing time: $t \approx 43.7 \, \text{s}$

## Interpretation

The boat must aim upstream to compensate for the river’s drift, ensuring a straight northward trajectory.
