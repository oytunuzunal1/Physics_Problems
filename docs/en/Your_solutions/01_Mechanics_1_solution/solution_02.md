# Task 02 – Range Optimization

## Problem Statement

Show that the projectile range

$$
R(\theta) = \frac{v_0^2 \sin(2\theta)}{g}
$$

is maximized at $\theta = 45^\circ$.

## Theory

The maximum of a function occurs where its derivative is zero.

## Step-by-Step Solution

Differentiate:

$$
\frac{dR}{d\theta} = \frac{v_0^2}{g} \cdot 2 \cos(2\theta)
$$

Set equal to zero:

$$
\cos(2\theta) = 0
$$

$$
2\theta = 90^\circ
$$

$$
\theta = 45^\circ
$$

## Final Result

Maximum range occurs at:

$$
\theta = 45^\circ
$$

## Interpretation

The symmetry of projectile motion ensures optimal balance between vertical and horizontal motion at $45^\circ$.
