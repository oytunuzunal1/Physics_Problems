# Task 03 – Path Intersection

## Problem Statement

Given:

$$
A(t) = (2+t, 8-3t)
$$

$$
B(t) = (2t-1, 2t+2)
$$

Determine whether the paths intersect.

## Theory

Intersection occurs when positions are equal:

$$
A(t) = B(t)
$$

## Step-by-Step Solution

Equate components:

$$
2 + t = 2t - 1
$$

$$
8 - 3t = 2t + 2
$$

Solve first:

$$
t = 3
$$

Check second:

$$
8 - 9 = -1
$$

$$
2(3) + 2 = 8
$$

Not equal → no collision.

## Final Result

Paths do not intersect.

## Interpretation

Although paths cross geometrically, the objects are not at the same position at the same time.
