# Task 08 – Circular Motion

## Problem Statement

Find centripetal acceleration at Earth's equator.

## Theory

$$
a_c = \omega^2 R
$$

$$
\omega = \frac{2\pi}{T}
$$

## Step-by-Step Solution

Earth rotation:

$$
T = 86400 \, \text{s}
$$

$$
R = 6.378 \times 10^6 \, \text{m}
$$

Angular velocity:

$$
\omega = \frac{2\pi}{86400}
$$

Centripetal acceleration:

$$
a_c = \omega^2 R
$$

$$
a_c \approx 0.034 \, \text{m/s}^2
$$

## Final Result

$$
a_c \approx 0.034 \, \text{m/s}^2
$$

## Interpretation

This acceleration is very small compared to gravity.
