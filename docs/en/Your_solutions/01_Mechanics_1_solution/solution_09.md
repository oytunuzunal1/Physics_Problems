# Task 09 – Momentum Comparison

## Problem Statement

Compare momentum of:

- Fly: $2 \, \text{g}$ at $10 \, \text{m/s}$
- Tennis ball: $60 \, \text{g}$ at $1 \, \text{m/s}$

## Theory

Momentum:

$$
p = mv
$$

## Step-by-Step Solution

Convert units:

$$
m_1 = 0.002, \quad m_2 = 0.06
$$

Fly:

$$
p_1 = 0.002 \cdot 10 = 0.02
$$

Ball:

$$
p_2 = 0.06 \cdot 1 = 0.06
$$

## Final Result

The tennis ball has greater momentum.

## Interpretation

Mass dominates over velocity in this comparison.
